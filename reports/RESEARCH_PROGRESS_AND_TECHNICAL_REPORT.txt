predicting 3x3 Born Effective Charge (BEC) tensors Z*_ij across the periodic 
table using an E(3)-equivariant Graph Neural Network (EquivarBECGNN).

================================================================================
2. MASTER DATASET CURATION & DEDUPLICATION RULES
================================================================================
1. Coordinate-Based Fingerprinting:
   - MD5 hash calculated from stoichiometry, atom count, and 3D atomic coordinates.
   - Polymorphic structural phases (e.g. cubic vs monoclinic ZrO2, thermal MD snapshots)
     with distinct atomic positions were preserved.
   - 34 exact coordinate duplicates were removed.

2. Acoustic Sum Rule (ASR) Neutrality Filter:
   - Net unit-cell charge drift calculated as ||sum_i Z*_i||_F.
   - 4,991 unphysical high-temperature MD snapshots with drift > 1.0 e were removed.

3. Insulator Requirement (Eg > 0 eV):
   - Verified Eg > 0 eV for all 38,857 retained structures.
