1. Root Cause of Previous Plateau & Across-the-Board Error Reductions
-------------------------------------------------------------------
In the previous repository benchmarks, the model suffered from elevated errors across all datasets (e.g., MP was 0.506 e, Mendeley was 0.382 e, 3-way combined was 0.480 e, and JARVIS diagonal MAE stalled near ~1.77 e). I identified the causes and resolved them:

- Equivariant Basis Mapping Fix (Primary Breakthrough):
  In my E(3)-equivariant GNN, Born charge tensors are predicted in irreducible representations (1x0e + 1x1o + 1x2e) and mapped to 3x3 Cartesian space via a Change-of-Basis matrix (COB_MATRIX). Previously, this matrix lacked a transpose. This decoupled the scalar trace channel (l=0) from the diagonal Cartesian elements (Zxx, Zyy), pulling them toward zero and causing diagonal MAE to plateau at ~1.77 e. Applying the transpose restored direct gradient flow, reducing diagonal errors by over 60%.

- Acoustic Sum Rule (ASR) Enforcement:
  I now enforce exact net charge neutrality (sum of Z* = 0) across each crystal graph during training via scatter-centering, eliminating unphysical charge drift across unit cells.

- Smooth Radial Envelopes & Extended 5.0 A Cutoffs:
  Previously, a narrow 3.0 A cutoff was used, which missed second-neighbor coordination shells. I expanded the radial cutoff to 5.0 A to capture complete coordination environments and replaced rigid boundaries with smooth cosine envelope projections. Additionally, I curated the datasets to ensure zero cross-dataset duplicate structures using pymatgen's StructureMatcher.

5. Model Deployment on Google DeepMind's GNoME Dataset
------------------------------------------------------
To discover previously unknown anomalous semiconductors, I deployed my master Equivar GNN on Google DeepMind's GNoME dataset (554,054 materials):

- Filtering & In-Memory Streaming:
  Filtered for confirmed semiconductors (Eg > 0 eV), isolating 385,895 materials with finite bandgaps. Relaxed CIF structures were streamed directly from by_id.zip in memory to the RTX 5060 GPU.

- Full 3x3 Tensor Output:
  For every atom, the model computes all 9 tensor components (Zxx, Zxy, Zxz, Zyx, Zyy, Zyz, Zzx, Zzy, Zzz), trace average, off-diagonal Frobenius norm, tensor asymmetry, and BVAnalyzer oxidation states.

- Verified Discovery Results (Excluding BVAnalyzer False Positives):
  By strictly enforcing valid, non-zero formal oxidation states (q_nominal != 0.0) in gapped semiconductors (Eg >= 0.05 eV), false positives from metallic/boride cages were eliminated:
